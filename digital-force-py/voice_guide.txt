# Voice Consistency Guide for Technical Writers

## Purpose
Maintain a single, recognizable brand voice across documentation, in‑app help, and marketing handoffs for complete user experience synergy.

## Core Voice Pillars
1. **Clear** – Use simple language, short sentences, and concrete verbs.
2. **Helpful** – Focus on the user’s problem first, then describe the solution.
3. **Friendly** – A conversational tone without being overly casual.
4. **Professional** – Maintain authority while being approachable.
5. **Inclusive** – Use gender‑neutral language and consider diverse audiences.

## Tone Mappings
| Surface | Primary Tone | Typical Constraints |
|---------|--------------|----------------------|
| Docs | Warm‑helpful | Avoid marketing buzzwords, use *you* pronoun, keep reviews and accessibility tones. |
| In‑App | Action‑oriented | Minimal space – keep sentences < 10 words, use imperative verbs, no jargon. |
| Marketing | Inspiring | High‑level benefits, aspirational language, occasional exclamation marks, no deep technical dives. |

## Voice In Context – Examples
| Situation | Docs | In‑App | Marketing |
|-----------|------|--------|----------|
| Installing | “To install the app, double‑click the downloaded file.” | “Click ‘Install’.” | “Get started with one click—install today.” |
| Error Message | “If you encounter error 404, verify your internet connection.” | “Network error. Check your connection.” | “Oops! It looks like something went wrong.” |

## Consistency Checklist (to repeat for each new piece)
1. **Core vocabulary** – Verify that key verbs, nouns, and adjectives match the style bank.
2. **Tone approval** – Quick “one‑liner” read‑through to confirm alignment with surface mapping.
3. **Formatting** – Docs: consistent heading levels, bold for key terms. In‑app: inline hints, placeholders. Marketing: bold calls‑to‑action.
4. **Feedback loop** – Share with Design and Marketing for cross‑surface flags.
5. **Version control** – Tag the document revision as *Voice‑Aligned*.

## Collaboration Workflow
1. **Document** – Draft in Markdown/Docx.
2. **Voice Pass‑through** – Paste the text into the *Voice Check** form (Google Sheet named `Voice_Consistent_Checks`).  An automated Google‑Script checks for banned words and structure.
3. **In‑App Injection** – Export snippet as JSON key/value, then merge into the translation file by the dev team.
4. **Marketing Handoff** – Attach the same source file to the project’s Slack channel and tag `#voice‑check`.
5. **Review** – After each release, audit one random doc / in‑app screen / marketing asset and update the guide if new patterns emerge.

## Quick Reference – Voice Bank
- *you* (user) / *they* (team) / *we* (company)
- “Check”, “fix”, “update”, “save”, “load” (action verbs)
- “quick”, “simple”, “straight‑forward” (adjectives for Simplicity)
- “essential”, “core”, “basic” (nouns for Priority)
